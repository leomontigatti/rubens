## What changed before the runs

- **The eval's turns.** The eval sent the patient's new messages without the
  `[new messages]` line that the prompt points at and production sends (`asTurnMessages`).
  It now builds them as production does (`turn.ts`). The time question judged a health
  question while a handoff was open (#300, #330: 1 of 3) has not failed since.
- **A proposal stated earlier is still stored.** On a yes, the model now calls
  `accept_proposal` without proposing again. The 👍 on a move proposal that re-proposed
  first (#330: 3 of 6) has not failed since.
- **The reply says nothing of what it hands off.** Run 1 said instead that underthing tells
  the patient about the handoff, and the knee and toothache replies then said `te pasé tu
  consulta` in 5 of 6 turns. Rewritten in run 2, they have not failed since.
- **A symptom given as the reason for a request it can handle** (moving because of the
  knee) is not a health question. Run 1 handed it off 2 of 3. It has not failed since.
- **During an open handoff, only the new messages are judged.** This didn't fix
  `confirm-during-reception-handoff`, but it is part of the prompt the confirmation run
  measured.
- Tried and reverted:
  - A new appointment handed off as `patient_asked` (run 2). It also handed off a question
    about Saturday times, 3 of 3.
  - "Reply with exactly" the open handoff's sentence. Review found it could drop a change
    the same turn made.

## The cases added

- `booking-for-a-toothache` and `toothache-medicine-with-dentist`: an appointment wanted
  because of a symptom goes to reception, and a question asking for advice goes to the
  practitioner who treats it. The dentist case is the one that checks the right practitioner
  is named, since the number has a kinesiologist and a dentist.
- `toothache-alone`: a health question no booked practitioner treats goes to reception, and
  the patient is told nothing.
- `soreness-after-reminder`: `Me duele un poco donde me atendieron` under a reminder goes
  to the practitioner (#183's sandbox miss).
- `confirm-during-reception-handoff` and `repeat-during-reception-handoff`: with a handoff to
  reception open, a new request is answered, and the open topic is neither answered nor
  handed off again.

## The open handoff's sentence (#362)

The user ruled on #359 that a repeat about any open handoff, reception's included, gets
`Ya se lo pasé, te va a responder por acá.` (`le va a responder` in usted). Two runs of Claude
Haiku 5.5, `--repeat 3`, 45 cases, 135 turns:

- **Prompt only.** The prompt asked the model for the sentence word for word, after any change.
  It wrote it in none of the 6 repeat turns (`repeat-during-reception-handoff` and the new
  `confirm-and-repeat-during-reception-handoff`, 0/3 each).
- **Code-side.** A turn that wants a handoff an open one already covers now settles as
  `alreadyHandedOff`. The model's reply goes first, checked, then underthing sends the fixed
  sentence. The model is told to say nothing of the topic. Both repeat cases passed 3/3. In the
  new case the model's reply was only the confirmation, and the sentence came after it.
- **Failures left in eval-Chd.**
  - `confirm-during-reception-handoff`, 3/3: the model judged the earlier toothache again, and
    the patient now gets `Ya se lo pasé` after a plain confirmation. Code can't tell this turn
    from a real repeat, so it is filed in #359 with the counts.
  - `thumbs-up-reaction-on-proposal`, 1/3: it proposed again before accepting.
- **After the run.** The review found that a repeat about a topic nothing judges (a request
  for a person) got no reply. Such a turn, with a handoff open and the model's reply empty,
  now gets the sentence too. The case `repeat-during-patient-asked-handoff` was added for it
  and has not run yet.
